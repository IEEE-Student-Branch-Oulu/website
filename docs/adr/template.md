# NNNN — Short title in sentence case

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names / roles

## Context

What forced this decision? What's the problem, the constraint, or the
opportunity? Two or three short paragraphs.

## Decision

What did we decide? One paragraph. Concrete and specific.

## Consequences

What becomes easier? What becomes harder? What new risks or commitments
does this create?

- Positive: …
- Negative / accepted cost: …
- Follow-ups required: …

## Alternatives considered

Brief notes on other options and why they lost. Don't write a thesis —
one or two sentences each.

- **Option A** — why not.
- **Option B** — why not.
